# MyTutor Privacy Policy (draft)

_Last updated: fill in before App Store submission._

MyTutor is a personal AI tutoring app. This draft covers the data practices
already built into the app; review and finalize it (and have it hosted at a
public URL) before submitting to the App Store.

## What we collect

- **Account**: email address, display name, optional handle, grade level,
  and program — provided by you at sign-up/onboarding.
- **Course content**: courses, curricula, and files you upload (PDFs,
  documents, photos, videos). Files are stored privately in your account's
  storage space.
- **Study activity**: tutoring conversations, session metrics (time,
  questions, hints), notes the tutor writes for you, study materials you
  create, assignments and grades you log.
- **Social** (optional): friendships you initiate and a weekly points/minutes
  summary visible to accepted friends on the leaderboard.

## How AI processing works

Conversations and uploaded materials are processed by Anthropic's Claude API
to provide tutoring, summaries, and study materials. Content is sent
server-side from our backend; your API traffic is subject to
[Anthropic's usage policies](https://www.anthropic.com/legal).

## What we don't do

- No ads, no sale of personal data, no third-party tracking SDKs.
- Your content is visible only to you (and the weekly leaderboard summary to
  accepted friends, if you use the friends feature).

## Your controls

- **Export**: download your entire notes vault from Settings at any time.
- **Delete**: Settings → "Delete account & all data" permanently removes your
  account, files, conversations, notes, metrics, and social data.
